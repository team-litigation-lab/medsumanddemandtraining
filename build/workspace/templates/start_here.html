TRAINING — SIMULATED CASE · fictional people and records

Dana Whitfield v. Keystone Mutual — your case workspace

This folder is your own copy of a real-looking file, exactly as it reaches a Demand Specialist: one PDF per provider or sender, unsorted, with fax and scanner names and no Bates numbers yet. Over the five days you'll organize it and build the work product, from the chronology to the demand letter, in Google Docs and Sheets.

Your trainer can open this folder at any time and see how you arranged it, and each document's version history shows how you built it. Work here, not in a copy on your own computer. When a day's work is ready, press Submit on the Case Workspace page in the training portal.

How the folder works

  • 01 Incoming — unsorted: everything that has come in so far. Empty it on Day 1.
  • 02 Case File: where every document belongs, one subfolder per category.
  • 03 Work Product: the documents you write (templates are already there).
  • 04 Received Wednesday: mail that comes in during the week. Don't open it until Day 3.
  • 05 Received after the demand: don't open it until Day 5.

Naming convention (LSH)

  • Every file: YYYY-MM-DD Source – Document, using the document's own date. Example: 2026-04-02 Keystone Mutual – Liability acceptance.pdf
  • Medical records, once you've put them in order: WHITFIELD 0001-0009 Riverside Medical Center – ED record 2026-03-14.pdf (the Bates range first, so the folder sorts in Bates order).
  • Never delete a file. Anything you won't use goes to 02 Case File / 09 Duplicates & not used, with the reason in your audit.

Day 1 — Organize the file

  1. Open every file in 01 Incoming and work out what it is, who sent it and its date.
  2. Rename it with the LSH convention and move it to the right 02 Case File subfolder.
  3. Put the medical records in Bates order: by provider, in the order Dana first saw each one after the crash, each provider's pages in order. Number them WHITFIELD 0001 onward and put each file's range at the front of its name.
  4. Check every core fact against the documents (date of incident, names, claim number) and record every problem you find in Whitfield — Day 1 File Audit: the issue, where it is (file and page) and what you'll do or who you'll tell.
  5. A bill that only shows a balance can't be itemized: note which itemized bills to request, and from whom.

Hand in: the organized folder and the Day 1 File Audit.

Day 2 — Medical chronology and medical summary

  1. Build Whitfield — Medical Chronology: one row per encounter, in date order, in the provider's words, with the Bates page for every row and a flag where one applies.
  2. Write Whitfield — Medical Summary from the chronology: neutral, factual, a Bates cite for every statement.

Hand in: the chronology and the medical summary.

Day 3 — Bills itemization

  1. Open 04 Received Wednesday: rename the bill that came in and file it in 02 Case File / 05 Medical Bills.
  2. Build Whitfield — Bills Itemization from the bills and ledgers: one line per charge, billed, adjustments, every payment by payer, balance and source.
  3. List what you left out (and why) on the Exclusions tab, and every balance or lien on the Balances & Liens tab.

Hand in: the itemization (the totals must tie out).

Day 4 — The demand letter

  1. Attorney Bennett's instructions (email of 10/08/2026): “Draft the Whitfield demand to Tom Reyes at Keystone. Use $85,000.00 and give them 30 days from the date it's received to respond. Address the January 2025 low back strain and the gap in treatment head-on. I'll review and sign.”
  2. Draft Whitfield — Demand Letter (draft) on the LSH structure. Every fact from the records gets an exhibit and Bates cite; every figure ties to your itemization; no opinions about value or the law.

Hand in: the demand letter draft.

Day 5 — The packet and the response

  1. Build Whitfield — Exhibit Index in the LSH standard order, with Bates ranges and page counts.
  2. In 02 Case File / 07 Demand Packet, add a shortcut to each exhibit in packet order (right-click → Organize → Add shortcut), named Ex. A – …, Ex. B – ….
  3. Now open 05 Received after the demand. File each letter in 08 Correspondence with the naming convention.
  4. Draft Whitfield — Reply to Keystone (draft): answer each point of Keystone's 11/04/2026 letter with the record that answers it. The attorney decides any counteroffer and the scope of the records request: flag those for her, don't decide them.

Hand in: the exhibit index, the packet folder and the reply draft.

Rules that never change

  • Cite the record for every fact: exhibit and Bates page.
  • Use the provider's words. Don't upgrade a diagnosis (a protrusion is not a herniation).
  • Value, the demand amount, offers, counteroffers and legal questions belong to Attorney Bennett. Liens and disbursement belong to Marcus Webb.
  • Client information stays in this folder. Don't download it to a personal device or share the folder.
